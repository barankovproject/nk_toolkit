| НК-1A-5 (and most widening canals) | Near-zero-length gabion sliver at the widening's far end: segment `to` PK (e.g. ПК0+80.51 → 80.510) ≠ alignment EndingStation (80.513), both added as stations → ~3 mm loft. | НК-3С-1 | `draw_gabion_view` crashed at `profile.ElevationAt(sta_start)` with `System.ArgumentException: значение не попадает в диапазон` — the profile does not span the alignment's StartingStation, and `ElevationAt` raises outside the profile's own domain. Fixed by `_profile_elev()` which clamps the query station into `[profile.StartingStation, profile.EndingStation]` (used for the bz datum and in `_section_pts`). | uncommitted |
| НК-2B-1 (near-horizontal / bending) | `draw_gabion_view` callout leaders crossed (13) and shelves overlapped (4) at sharp bends: side/offset was decided in world X and the fan spread in a per-station world projection (non-monotonic on a bend), and a fixed offset locus folds where the shelf offset exceeds the bend radius. Fixed by a placement solver in `layout.py`: side in the transverse frame (u = P6→P5); fan on the **centreline arc** (monotone ⇒ no crossing) with a per-pair gap scaled by 1/compression; label placed on the centreline at its fanned arc, offset by `o` along the **interpolated transverse u**; then a **feedback loop widens any pair whose real shelf boxes still collide** until they clear (изломы spread along the legs with long leaders, доборные past the budget are dropped). Regression locked by `test_no_leader_crossings_on_real_bent_canal` + `test_no_shelf_overlaps_on_real_bent_canal`. Verified 0/0 offline on 2B-1, 1А-5, 3С-1, 1А-7. | uncommitted |
| НК-1A-6 | `draw_gabion_view` offset-rail placement: callouts т.33/т.35 swapped (crossed leaders) mid-left side. The loop rotation seam (`order = seq[r:] + seq[:r]`, r = mid-left) landed exactly between two callout stations only 0.73 m apart (55.752 / 56.483); the monotone spread has no gap constraint across the chain ends, so the seam pair was pushed *through* each other (start backward, end forward → inversion). Fixed by rotating the seam to the **largest rail gap between same-side neighbours** instead of blindly mid-left. | 90b5de6 |
| НК-1А-3 | `draw_gabion_view` offset-rail placement: `rail offset produced nothing` → silent fallback to fan, callouts off the rail. The outline loop is clean (322 verts, 0 self-intersections), but `GetOffsetCurves` with OFFSETGAPTYPE=1 (fillet) returns **0 curves at any distance** on the dense outline — arc insertion chokes on nearly-collinear vertices (gap types 0/2 work; decimated outline works). Confirmed via `debug/dump_rail_offset`. Fixed by `_decimate()` — dropping vertices within 1 cm of the running chord before offsetting (322 → 48 verts; real corners survive). | 1707c27 |
| НК-1E-2 | `build_canal_model` lofted a 0.109 m gabion sliver (FillVolume 0.33 m³) at sta 547.7: PVI 547.678 and plan PI 547.787 are 0.109 m apart — just over the 0.1 m station snap, so both survived as loft stations, and the tail-merge can't help a span that has only 2 stations. Fix: drop PVIs within `GABION.pi_merge_tol` (0.5 m) of a plan PI in `_build_stations` (the PI wins, keeping the bisector miter) — mirrors the existing trough_builder convention. | 253bd3d |
| НК-1А-3 | `build_canal_model`: the gabion unit right after plan PI 276.104 (39.24°, type 6) has a **3.21 m outer edge** — the bisector face's points advance by the miter **reach** = pullback (`half_top_w·tan(φ/2)` ≈ 1.23 m) + top-wall lean (`d·|Δ(slope/mag)|` when a grade break sits on the PI), so a full 2 m step next to the PI stretches past 3 m. Requirement: every longitudinal gabion edge ≤ 2 m (standard ГСИ basket). For type 6 at 39° the corner-piece window `[reach+0.1, (2−0.05−reach)/mag]` is **empty** — a bisector cut cannot satisfy both no-self-intersection and the 2 m cap. Fix: (a) reserve PI-adjacent corner pieces via `corner_piece_len(reach, mag)` (mag refined mid-piece); (b) when the window is empty, trigger the **virtual arc fan** geometrically in `_find_sharp_pis` (was: only types 7-8 ≥ 15°) — fan slices keep outer edges ≤ loft_step by construction. Verified by `automation/tools/check_edge_lengths.py --pi-only`: 0 violations on 1А-3/3А-1/1A-5 (was 49/58 canals fleet-wide). | uncommitted |
| НК-3А-1 | `build_canal_model` left a visible gap at plan PI 133.562 (39.37°): the miter wedge span 133.049→133.562 (0.513 m) is shorter than the bisector pullback `half_top_w·tan(φ/2) ≈ 0.80 m`, so the inner-side profile points move *backwards* (P8 dx = −0.236 m), the loft self-intersects and AutoCAD silently produces a degenerate 0.017 m³ solid (handle 890C86) instead of the wedge — `errors=0` in the log. Confirmed via `debug/dump_pi_gap`. Fix: PI-adjacent sub-spans must be ≥ miter pullback (+margin) — extend the tail-merge minimum from fixed 0.5 m to `max(0.5, pullback + 0.1)` on spans that end/start at a PI. | 253bd3d |
